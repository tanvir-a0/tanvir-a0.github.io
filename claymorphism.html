The image was captured from SATGUS (NORAD ID: 62713), launched on January 14, 2025 aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 (Transporter 12 mission) from Vandenberg Space Force Base, California. SATGUS is in Low Earth Orbit, initially at an altitude of approximately 375 miles above Earth.

The HDR image was taken using an Argus camera by Redwire Space, featuring fixed 3 mm f/8 lens which is capable of capturing a horizontal ground swath of over 600 miles at that altitude. My image was displayed on a Google Pixel phone display in orbit, and the selfie was captured by the Argus camera.
