1st Year University Project

Small Firefighting Robot

A small, Bluetooth-controlled firefighting robot created as a 1st year, 1st semester final project.

This project was an outcome of our "Machine shop and Workshop Practices Lab", where we applied CNC milling and lathe techniques.

Role Team Member, Fabricator
Toolchain CNC Milling, CNC Lathe, Soldering, Arduino
View Gallery

About The Project

We are building a fire fighting robot which includes soldering operations, CNC turning and CNC milling operations which were previously performed in our course machine workshop and practices lab. The main purpose of this robot is to extinguish fire by launching water with a pump located on top of it.

In this project, we tried our best to avoid using any type of ready made products . From structure to different joints, most of the components of the robot were made using the operations which were performed by us in our respective lab course. The bottom slab containing the motors in pockets were designed by CNC milling. The motors are connected to the wheels. Soldering was done while designing the Arduino circuit. The upper plate is connected with the lower one using four round pillars which were designed using a CNC turning machine. Two servo motors were used for the rotation of the launcher in different axes. One of them can rotate 180 degrees about the x axis and the other about the z axis. A pump is placed on top of the robot to launch water.
