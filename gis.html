Geographic Information Systems

GIS Projects

Geographic Information Systems (GIS) combine the power of spatial data analysis with programming to solve environmental and geographical challenges. My GIS work leverages satellite imagery, Python programming, and cloud-based platforms to create meaningful insights about our planet.

From analyzing Bangladesh's topography to creating satellite timelapses showing environmental changes, these projects demonstrate the potential of GIS technology in environmental monitoring and geospatial analysis.
