Computational Fluid Dynamics

CFD Projects

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) is a powerful tool for analyzing and optimizing fluid flow, heat transfer, and thermal management in engineering designs. My CFD work focuses on simulating real-world scenarios to improve performance and efficiency of robotic systems and mechanical designs.

Using industry-standard software like Autodesk CFD and ANSYS, I've conducted simulations for various applications including aerodynamics, thermal analysis, and flow optimization in robotics.

CFD Analysis & Simulations

My CFD projects involve detailed analysis of fluid behavior, thermal characteristics, and optimization of designs for better performance. Each simulation helps inform design decisions and validate theoretical concepts.
