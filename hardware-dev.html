Hardware development is the foundation of any physical computing system. From designing custom PCBs to working with microcontrollers and embedded systems, I focus on creating robust, efficient hardware solutions that bring digital concepts into the physical realm.

My hardware expertise spans PCB design, embedded systems programming, sensor integration, and working with various microcontroller platforms including ESP32, STM32, Arduino, and more.
