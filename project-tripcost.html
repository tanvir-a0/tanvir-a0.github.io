TripCost: Group Expense Tracker

TripCost is a comprehensive expense management application designed to simplify group trip finances. Track who pays for what, monitor individual contributions, and automatically calculate who owes money to whom—all in real-time with Firebase synchronization.

Eliminates manual calculation errors and makes group expense tracking effortless for trips with friends and family.

Role Full Stack Developer
Platform Android & iOS
Backend Firebase Firestore

Group Management

Create trip groups effortlessly and invite friends to join. Each group maintains its own expense tracking, making it perfect for multiple simultaneous trips or events.

Real-time Dashboard

Get instant insights into your trip expenses with a comprehensive dashboard that shows total spending, individual contributions, and current balances at a glance.

Expense Tracking

Record every expense with detailed information about who paid, who benefited, and what it was for. Track money you spent on others and money others spent on you separately for clarity.

Automatic Settlement

After the trip, TripCost automatically calculates who owes money to whom, eliminating manual calculations and reducing errors. Get a clear breakdown of all debts and credits.

Member Overview

View all group members, their individual spending patterns, and contribution history. Perfect for keeping everyone accountable and transparent.

Personal Net Balance

Instantly see your net position in the group—whether you're owed money or need to pay others. All calculations are done automatically and updated in real-time.

Project Overview

TripCost was born from a personal need during group trips with friends. Managing expenses for 15 people traveling to Nepal revealed how complex and error-prone manual tracking can be—who paid for lunch, dinner, bus tickets, flights, and accommodations quickly became overwhelming.

The application solves this by providing a centralized platform where every expense is logged with details about the payer, beneficiaries, and purpose. Whether you're splitting a restaurant bill, covering accommodation for the group, or buying individual items, TripCost handles it all.

Built with Flutter for cross-platform compatibility and Firebase Firestore for real-time synchronization, the app ensures all group members see updates instantly.

Why TripCost?

The Problem: Manual expense tracking during group trips is tedious, error-prone, and often leads to confusion about who owes whom.

The Solution: A mobile application that automates expense tracking, provides real-time updates, and calculates optimal settlements.

  • Eliminates manual calculation errors
  • Provides instant visibility into group finances
  • Simplifies post-trip settlements
  • Maintains complete expense history
  • Works offline with automatic sync when connected

Key Features

TripCost is packed with features designed to make group expense management seamless and stress-free.

Easy Group Setup

Create new trip groups in seconds with a simple interface. Invite members via unique group codes, and everyone's expenses are automatically synchronized across all devices.

Flexible Joining Options

Join existing groups using unique codes shared by group creators. Perfect for last-minute additions or for friends who download the app mid-trip.

Bidirectional Tracking

Track both money you spent on others and money others spent on you. This dual perspective ensures complete transparency and makes verification easy.

Intuitive Navigation

Access all features quickly with a well-designed navigation system. Dropdown menus and contextual actions make expense entry and review effortless.

Core Capabilities

  • Multi-group support for managing multiple trips simultaneously

  • Automatic settlement calculations minimizing transaction count
